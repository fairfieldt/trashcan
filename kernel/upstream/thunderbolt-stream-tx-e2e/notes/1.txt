Testing: the tests in the message ran on 7.2.9 (results with and without
this change; without it 0 of 10 stream opens succeeded and the
controller had to be reset).  The same change has been in the 7.3-rc7
kernels of six Mac Pro 6,1s since 14 October, which use USB4STREAM
links between each other daily.  On 27 October it also ran on this
base: mainline 8a36f53fd5ec (the 7.4 merge window, with thunderbolt.git
next merged) on two of those Macs, one Thunderbolt 2 cable between
them.  With the patch, 500 of 500 back-to-back sessions (normal exits,
killed clients, server restarts; with and without busy_poll) reached
their stream within 100 ms, and 5 MiB transfers ran at 580-630 MB/s
each way.  With the same kernel and only this patch reverted in
thunderbolt_stream.ko, 0 of 7 sessions got any data across the stream
(each gave up after 60 s).
On this base it builds with W=1 and adds no sparse warnings.

The patch applies to thunderbolt.git next and to mainline; it doesn't
apply to the current fixes branch, which predates the RX polling change.

debugged the failure with me and wrote the patch and this message; the
hardware tests ran on my machines.
