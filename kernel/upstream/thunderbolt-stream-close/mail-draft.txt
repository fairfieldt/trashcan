Hi Mika,

I use USB4STREAM between six Mac Pro 6,1s (Falcon Ridge, software
connection manager), one stream per Thunderbolt 2 cable, opened and
closed many times a day.  Two things to report, one question.

1. On 7.3, since e120d14d03b3 ("thunderbolt: stream: Add support for busy
   polling"), once a stream device has read a CLOSE every later read
   returns end of file and every write fails with -ENXIO until the device
   is closed.  On these hosts a CLOSE sent at the end of one session can
   reach the other end's next session: the receiving controller holds a
   frame until about three more arrive behind it, so a CLOSE written
   while the other end is still open, or closing, can be delivered after
   it has opened again.  The next session then fails at once.  Measured
   on one cable, 20 back-to-back sessions: 9 reached their stream within
   100 ms on 7.3-rc7, 192 of 200 with a local patch that restores 7.2's
   behaviour (the closed check only where a blocking read or write would
   otherwise wait).

2. On the 7.4 merge window (mainline 8a36f53fd5ec) I can't reproduce it,
   and the frame hold is gone: a single frame with nothing behind it
   arrives at once (0.2-0.3 ms for two lone frames' round trip, 0.04-0.07
   ms with busy_poll, against a 20 ms wait on 7.3).  With the RX ring
   polled and the release consuming a CLOSE already in the ring, 500 of
   500 sessions reached their stream within 100 ms, and 80 rounds of "a
   CLOSE sent to an end that is open and not reading, then a new session
   at both ends" all started clean (no EPOLLHUP, no end of file, data
   read, write ok), with blocking and non-blocking I/O and busy_poll on
   and off.  So I don't think 7.4 needs a change, and the CLOSE semantics
   from 8d55710a8c43 work for me.

Question: is the 7.3 behaviour worth fixing in 7.3.y?  I haven't
bisected what removed the hold in 7.4; the RX ring polling (20bea8ceeb6a)
and the tb_ring_poll() descriptor changes are the likely candidates, and
they look too large for stable.  So it would be a 7.3-only patch along
the lines of the one above, naming them.  If you'd take that, I'll send it; if 7.3 users of
USB4STREAM are few enough that it isn't worth it, I'll keep it local
until I move to 7.4.

A separate observation from the same day, on 7.3-rc7: a host whose peer
rebooted (the peer's firmware XDomain appeared, then "failed read
XDomain properties", then two more disconnects over the next half hour)
oopsed in tb_handle_hotplug() -> kobject_get() on the next disconnect
and panicked.  I have the full log and will send a separate report once
I can reproduce it on 7.4.
