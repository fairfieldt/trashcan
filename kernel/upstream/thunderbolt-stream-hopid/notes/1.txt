Testing: compile-tested on this base (W=1, no new sparse warnings).  Not
run on hardware: I didn't want to provoke HopID conflicts on the hosts I
have, as getting there involves wedging a host router.  Instead the
HopID functions of stream.c (tbstream_dev_release(),
tbstream_dev_alloc_in/out_hopid(), tbstream_dev_attach_stream() and
tbstream_dev_detach_stream()) were compiled unchanged in userspace
against a model of the XDomain HopID IDAs, and driven through attach,
HopID changes, detach and release with allocations that fail or find
the HopID taken.  Before this patch, five of six scenarios free a HopID
the stream doesn't hold (ida_free() of an unallocated ID, or another
user's ID); after it none do and nothing is left allocated.

Open question: when an allocation fails on attach the configured HopID
is now forgotten (reads back as 0) rather than kept for a later attach.
Keeping it would need a separate "allocated" flag; I went for the
smaller change.

How this was made: an AI coding assistant (Claude, in Claude Code) found
the cause by reading the code after the warning, and wrote the patch,
the userspace test and this message at my direction.
