Measure prefill and decode without stopping on end-of-turn tokens

Usage: trashcan bench [OPTIONS] <MODEL>

Arguments:
  <MODEL>  

Options:
      --pp <PP>                      [default: 512]
      --tg <TG>                      [default: 128]
      --batch <BATCH>                [default: 512]
      --profile <PROFILE>            
      --draft <DRAFT>                
      --draft-tokens <DRAFT_TOKENS>  [default: 3]
      --progress                     Report each prefill chunk and ordinary decode step, checking finite top scores
  -h, --help                         Print help
