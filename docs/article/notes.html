Where the numbers come from

Back to the article

Paths are in the trashcan repository. Unless a note says otherwise, measurements are from the six-Mac chain with the G-iq4 recipe and --head-split 12 --context 32768 --batch 128 --ngram disk, with every GPU at 850 MHz core / 1370 MHz memory while computing. “Calculated” means arithmetic on measured or specified values; “modeled” means a projection from a cost model; neither is a benchmark.

  1. Hardware. Mac Pro 6,1 (late 2013, so 12 years 10 months old at publication; “nearly 13”). Two AMD FirePro D700 per Mac, each 6 GiB GDDR5 on a 384-bit bus, Apple-rated 264 GB/s and 3.5 TFLOP/s; clock-derived peaks 263.04 GB/s and 3.48 TFLOP/s FP32. The two cards’ memories are separate; GPU-to-GPU traffic goes through the CPU root complex, and only 256 MB of each card is CPU- or peer-visible. Interconnect: Thunderbolt 2. docs/hardware.md, docs/engine-spec.md.
  2. Model. Qwen3.8-Flash-Next (qwen4exp): 177B parameters, of which 121B routed experts (~6B used per token), a 51B hashed n-gram embedding read at layer 1, and 4.6B dense. 48 layers: 36 Gated DeltaNet, 12 Qwen Sparse Attention (every fourth, layers 3, 7, …, 47). 512 experts, top 10. Vocabulary 248,320. “180B” is 177B rounded. docs/qwen4.md.
  3. Quality. G-iq4 (experts IQ4_XS gate/up, IQ4_NL down; dense matrices Q8_0; head Q6_K) against the Q8_0 release: mean KL divergence 0.0391, same top token 95.1%, perplexity ratio 1.002, over 39K tokens of mixed text (19 chunks of 2,048), llama.cpp on CPU. This is fidelity to the 8-bit model, not a capability benchmark. results/qwen4/quant-20261006.
  4. Weights. G-iq4: 116 GiB, 51 GiB of it the n-gram table (Q8_0), which stays on the driver’s SSD and is read through the page cache (0.40 ms p50 per token, 16 readers). 65 GiB = 116 − 51 (calculated). 1.4 GiB per layer = 65 ÷ 48 (calculated average; the 65 GiB also includes the embedding and output head, and attention layers differ from DeltaNet layers). A Mac’s two stages load 11.8 GB. docs/qwen4.md (N-gram table placement, Quantization, Recommendation), results/qwen4/ngram-20261006.
  5. GPU memory. incite GPU 0, layers 0–3 plus the first twelfth of the head (rows 0–21,503), 32K context, 128-token chunks: 5,830 MiB allocated of a 5,888 MiB device-local heap, driver budget 5,869 MiB (measured). 39 MiB = 5,869 − 5,830 (calculated). A 6,480 MiB stage spilled to system memory and ran its head 18× slower. docs/qwen4.md (VRAM).
  6. Fewer Macs. From tools/qwen4_budget.py, which matches 15 measured stages within 0.2 MiB (modeled): five Macs put 5 layers on most GPUs, 106 MiB over even with 3-bit experts; four Macs fit with IQ2_XXS/MXFP4 experts (KLD ≥ 0.21, against 0.196 for every dense matrix at Q4_0). docs/qwen4.md (Quantization, VRAM).
  7. Links. Residual payload 4 × 2,560 f32 = 40,960 B per token. 34 µs = 40,960 × 8 ÷ 9.6 Gb/s, line rate only, no protocol overhead (calculated). Measured on a loopback cable: 25 µs round trip for 64 B over USB4STREAM, 9.6–9.7 Gb/s one way per cable; thunderbolt_net with throttling=32000: 40 µs. Ring order incite, inflight, alight, indite, invite, enlight (2026-10-14). docs/kernel.md, results/kernel/tb-loopback-20261006, results/qwen4/main-suite-20261013.
  8. Battery power state. Stock amdgpu left both D700s at 300 MHz core / 150 MHz memory under load (about 9× less memory bandwidth): the second GPU got the wrong VBIOS and the cards stayed in the battery power state. Fixed by an exact-match VFCT VBIOS fix and a backported SI power-source patch. docs/hardware.md (Clock bug).

  10. Ceilings (modeled). A decode token reads about 6.0 GB. Bandwidth ceiling: all bytes at 263 GB/s, one GPU at a time = 21.2 ms, 47 tok/s at 512 tokens of context. Practical: about 205 GB/s measured streaming rate per D700 plus ~4.5 ms of host work, “near 31 tok/s”. docs/qwen4.md (Projected chain speed), results/qwen4/decode-kernels-20261014.
  11. Prompt reading. Main suite on 15e5457, 2026-10-14 11:17: 522.9 / 523.3 tok/s at 512, 889.8 at 2,048, 1,118.0 at 8,192, 1,249.3 at 30,000. Times = length ÷ rate (calculated). The first chain on 2026-10-12 read a 512-token prompt at 126–132 tok/s. results/qwen4/main-suite-20261013, results/qwen4/chain-20261012.
  12. Speculative decoding. MTP drafting on the driver’s CPU, verification on the chain: chat replies 30.05 → 31.28 tok/s with the same tokens as plain decoding, 2026-10-14. results/qwen4/decode-kernels-20261014, docs/qwen4.md.
  13. Serving. trashcan serve: “requests run serially through one resident engine”, one resident sequence with prefix reuse. docs/server.md, docs/qwen4.md (Serving).
  14. Power. Session 3 on main 4307169, 2026-10-13, SMC sensors over the decode windows (variant without the CPU performance-governor hold): 20.61 tok/s, six Macs’ PSTR 1,357–1,364 W, CPU packages 340–343 W, GPUs 730–735 W. “Everything else” = 1,360.5 − 341.5 − 732.5 = 286.5 W (calculated from midpoints). 15,150 tok/s per MW = 20.61 ÷ 1,360.5 W × 106 (calculated, same run). Waiting with the engine running (idle cores busy-polling): 320–350 W for six Macs. results/qwen4/decode-host-20261013, docs/qwen4.md (Serving).
  15. Power events. 2026-10-09: the strip feeding four Macs tripped during a long prefill at ~1.65 kW sustained (results/qwen/tp8-mesh-20261009). 2026-10-14 11:04:53: all six Macs and the workstation lost power about 30 s after the six reached ~1.45–1.5 kW; cause not confirmed (results/qwen4/main-suite-20261013, power-loss-20261014-pstr.csv).
  16. Scaling (modeled). Four Macs with experts in host RAM and ~63% of each layer’s experts cached in VRAM: ~10.8 tok/s decode (not built). Tensor or expert parallelism over all 12 GPUs: ~2 ms of bytes per token, ~96 cross-Mac all-reduces per token at ≥100 µs, bounding it near 50–80 tok/s. Fan: fancurve.service holds 2,350 RPM or the SMC maximum. docs/qwen4.md, docs/hardware.md.

About the parody

The structure and premise follow Cerebras’s How we deploy trillion-parameter frontier models on Cerebras: split a model too large for one device across a pipeline of devices, then size it. The text and figures here are original; nothing from the original post is reproduced. No new benchmarks were run for this page.
